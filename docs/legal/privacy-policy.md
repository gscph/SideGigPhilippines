## Sa madaling salita (In short)

- **Ano ang kinukuha namin:** pangalan mo, mobile number, email (kung Google ang gamit mo), mga sagot mo sa app,
  mga photo na ina-upload mo, ang mga chat at review mo. Ang eksaktong location mo, **kapag pinindot mo lang** ang
  "Gamitin ang location ko" (hindi ito nakikita ng iba: bayan at ilang km lang), o kapag customer ka at pinindot mo
  ang "I-send ang location ko" para sa worker na pinili mo (siya lang ang makakakita, at buburahin pag tapos o
  na-cancel). **Hindi namin kinukuha** ang contacts mo, o ang GCash/bank mo.
- **Bakit:** para mahanap ng customer ang Skilled Worker, para makapag-usap kayo, at para iwas-scam (isang number,
  isang account).
- **Sino ang nakakakita:** ang listing ng Skilled Worker (pangalan, photo, trabaho, bayan, presyo, review) ay
  **public sa lahat ng user**. Ang chat ay kayong dalawa lang.
  **Private ang mobile number** ng worker at ng customer. Kapag pinili ng customer ang offer ng worker sa post
  niya, may **Call** at **Text** na sa chat: hindi ipinapakita ng app ang number, pero makikita ito sa sariling
  tawag o text app ng phone mo.
- **Kung gusto mo ng badge** (ID, NBI o police clearance, TESDA, dating project): optional ito at kailangan ng pagpayag mo. Private ang
  photo, tao sa Tarbaho lang ang titingin, at **buburahin pagkatapos i-check**. Ang ID ay binabasa ng AI (pangalan,
  ID number, expiry) para tulungan ang nagche-check. Hindi ito ipinapakita sa customer.
- **Hindi namin binebenta ang data mo.** Walang ads.
- **Pwede mong burahin lahat:** Me → Delete account. O mag-email sa jcadiao1102@gmail.com.

### 3.3 Our own usage counts (no analytics or advertising tools)

The App has **no third-party analytics, A/B testing or advertising tools** (no Google Analytics), and we do not
collect your advertising ID. We count a few things ourselves, in our own database, to answer two questions: do
people find the service they need, and do they come back.

- **Searches:** when your search results load, the App notes the trade, your town, the words you typed (numbers and
  emails are taken out first) and how many workers were found. This is saved on your phone first and sent to our
  server later in the background, never while you are searching.
- **What happens on our server:** when you sign up, post what you need, pick a worker's offer, finish a job, publish
  your listing, or report or block someone, our server notes it (with the trade, the town, and for a report its
  reason).
- Each count carries your **account ID** (a random code, not your name or number) so we can see whether people come
  back; never your name, number, email, address, location or messages.
- Counts are kept **12 months**. When you delete your account, your account ID is removed from them.
- We use them only to improve Tarbaho (for example, which services are missing in which town). We never sell them or
  use them for ads.

### 3.5 Badge proofs (government ID, clearance, certificates, proof of work)

Sending a proof is **optional** and happens only with your **consent**: the screen explains what happens and you
tick a box before your phone's photos open. Without the tick, nothing is sent.

- **Government ID:** our provider's AI (Microsoft Azure AI Document Intelligence) reads the **name, ID number and
  expiry date** from the photo, only to help the person at Tarbaho who checks it. The AI does not decide anything.
  A person compares the ID with your profile photo and approves or declines. We do **not** take a selfie for
  matching and do **not** use face recognition.
- **NBI or police clearance:** checked by a person at Tarbaho only. No AI reads it. A clearance can show
  information about offences, which the law treats as sensitive: we use it only to decide the badge. We approve a
  genuine clearance in your name that is recent (NBI under 1 year, police under 6 months) and shows no record. If
  we decline one, only you see the reason, and we never show or keep what was on it.
- **TESDA certificate, proof of a past project:** checked by a person at Tarbaho only. No AI reads them.
- **Photo check:** an ID or a clearance is photographed with your camera at that moment (not picked from your
  gallery). When any proof arrives, our server measures only how sharp and how bright the photo is (no AI, nothing
  is read from it) so it can say right away if it looks blurry, dark or has glare. That is only advice: you can take
  it again (the new photo replaces the old one, which is deleted) or keep it. We keep those measurements and, if a
  proof is declined, the kind of reason (for example "blurry"), to make the check better. They say nothing about you.
- The photo is kept in **private storage** (not at a web address), can be opened only by our authorised checkers,
  and **every time one is opened it is logged**.
- When the check is done (normally within 2 days), the **photo and everything read from it are deleted**. We keep
  only the result: which badge you hold, the company and building you named, and when it was decided.
- Customers never see the photo, your ID number or anything read from it. They see only the badge.

## 4. Why we use your data (lawful basis)

Under Sections 12 and 13 of the DPA, we process your data because:

1. **It is needed to provide the App you asked for** (contract): sign-in, your listing, search, chats,
   notifications, reviews.
2. **Our legitimate interests**, balanced against your rights: preventing fraud and fake accounts (one number per
   account), keeping the App safe, ranking search results, fixing errors, improving the
   list of jobs and trades, and counting how the App is used (§3.3).
3. **Your consent**: optional data (Customer profile photo, income goal), push notifications and error reports.
   You can withdraw consent at any time in the App or by emailing us; this does not affect
   what was done before.
4. **Legal obligations**: answering lawful orders of courts, the NPC, the DTI and law enforcement.

We do **not** sell or rent your personal data, we do not show ads, and we do not make decisions about you based
solely on automated processing that produce legal effects. Search ranking uses only listing signals (job match,
town, reviews, how fast a Skilled Worker replies, how complete the listing is); see §6.

## 7. How we protect your data

- All traffic between the App and our server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data is encrypted at rest by our providers.
- The App never connects to our database directly. Every request is checked against your sign-in token, and you
  can only read or change your own private data.
- Uploaded photos have their hidden metadata (EXIF, including any GPS location) removed: most are re-encoded by our
  server; a Skilled Worker's selfie is made small on the phone and our server takes that metadata out again before
  storing it.
- Badge proofs (IDs, certificates, proof of work) are kept in private storage that has no public web address, are
  opened only by authorised checkers with every look logged, and are deleted once checked.
- Only authorised staff can access production data, and only when needed.
- If a personal data breach happens that is likely to harm you, we will notify the NPC and affected users within
  **72 hours** of discovering it, as required by NPC rules.

No system is perfectly secure. Protect your phone and never share your SMS code with anyone, including people who
say they are from Tarbaho.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPA you have the right to:

1. **Be informed** — this Policy.
2. **Access** — ask what data we hold about you and get a copy.
3. **Correct** (rectification) — most of your data can be edited in the App.
4. **Erase or block** — delete your account in the App (**Me → Delete account**), or ask us.
5. **Object** — to processing based on legitimate interest or consent, including error reports.
6. **Data portability** — get your data in a commonly used electronic format.
7. **Damages** — be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained
   or unauthorised use of your data.
8. **File a complaint** with the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph, complaints@privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, email **jcadiao1102@gmail.com** and tell us the mobile number of your account. We may
ask you to confirm the number with an SMS code. We answer within **5 business days**.
