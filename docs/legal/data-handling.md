# How Tarbaho Handles Your Personal Data

**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

This is the plain-language companion to the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), and where to **delete your account
and data**, with or without the App.

---

## 1. Ano ang alam namin tungkol sa iyo (What we know about you)

| Ano (What) | Sino ang nakakakita (Who sees it) | Gaano katagal (How long) |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number | **Skilled Worker:** lahat ng user, kapag naka-publish ang listing mo. **Customer:** kami lang, at ang Skilled Worker na tinawagan o tinext mo | Hanggang i-delete mo ang account |
| Pangalan (Name) | Ang mga kausap mo; lahat ng user kung Skilled Worker ka; maikling pangalan sa review ("Maricel D.") | Hanggang i-delete mo ang account |
| Email (Google sign-in lang) | Kami lang | Hanggang i-delete mo ang account |
| Profile photo / selfie | Lahat ng user (Skilled Worker); ang kausap mo (Customer) | Hanggang palitan o i-delete mo |
| Work photos | Lahat ng user | Hanggang tanggalin mo o i-delete ang account |
| Trabaho, bayan, presyo, experience (Listing) | Lahat ng user, habang naka-publish | Hanggang i-unpublish o i-delete mo |
| References (pangalan at number), kung nagbigay ka bago Nov 18, 2026. Hindi na namin hinihingi o ginagamit ang mga ito | **Kami lang. Hindi kailanman ipinapakita kaninuman** | Hanggang i-delete mo ang account, o burahin namin lahat |
| Chat messages | Kayong dalawa lang. Titingnan lang namin kapag may nag-report, may utos ng korte, o para pigilan ang scam | Hanggang i-delete ng isa sa inyo ang account |
| Reviews | Lahat ng user | Hanggang i-delete ang account ng sumulat o ng ni-review |
| Bayan mo (hindi GPS) | Ginagamit sa search | Hanggang palitan mo |
| "Others" na tinype mo | Kami lang, pang-statistics | Natatanggal ang pangalan mo kapag nag-delete ka ng account |
| Feature na sinuggest mo at mga boto mo | Lahat ng naka-sign in, pero walang pangalan mo | Naiiwan ang suggestion na walang pangalan mo; nabubura ang mga boto mo |
| Error reports | Kami lang. **Walang pangalan, number o account** | 90 araw |

**Hindi namin kinukuha:** GPS location, contacts, call log, SMS, GCash o bank details, advertising ID.
**Hindi namin binebenta** ang data mo, at walang ads sa app.

## 2. Where your data is stored

- **Microsoft Azure, Southeast Asia (Singapore):** database, photos, server.
- **Google Firebase (USA and other Google locations):** sign-in (SMS codes, Google sign-in) and new-message
  notifications.
- Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Photos have hidden location data (EXIF/GPS) removed when
  uploaded.

## 3. Choices you have in the App

| Gusto mo (You want to) | Pumunta sa (Go to) |
|---|---|
| Palitan ang photo o work photos | **Me** |
| Burahin ang listing at mga sagot mo, pero hindi ang account | Me → **Start over** |
| Patayin ang notifications | Phone settings → Apps → Tarbaho → Notifications |
| Huwag magpadala ng error reports | Me → **Send error reports** (off) |
| Mag-report o mag-block ng user | Sa profile, review o chat → 🚩 **Report or block** |
| Burahin ang lahat | Me → **Delete account** |

## 4. Delete your account and data

### In the App (fastest)

1. Open Tarbaho and go to **Me**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

### Without the App

Email **jcadiao1102@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my account"** and the **mobile number** of your account. We may
send an SMS code to that number to make sure the request is really from you. We delete the account within
**5 business days** and email you when it is done.

### What gets deleted

- your sign-in account (mobile number, email, name);
- your profile, listing, profile photo and work photos, and any references you gave before November 18, 2026;
- every chat you are in, with its messages (for both people in the chat);
- reviews you wrote, and reviews about you;
- the people you blocked, and who blocked you;
- your notification tokens.

### What is kept, and for how long

| Data | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| "Others" text you typed | Statistics on which jobs to add, **unlinked from you** | Kept |
| Features you suggested | Shown on the board **without your name**, unlinked from you | Kept (your votes are deleted) |
| Error reports | Fixing crashes; never linked to your account | Up to 90 days after they were sent |
| Reports other users made **about** you | Handling the report | Until it is resolved |
| Reports you made | Handling the report, **without your name** | Until it is resolved |
| Database backups | Disaster recovery | Up to 35 days, then overwritten |
| Data needed for a legal claim, a report under investigation, or a legal order | Required by law | Only as long as needed |
