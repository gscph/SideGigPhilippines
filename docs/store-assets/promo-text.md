# Tarbaho: store and promo text

Store and launch text for the two sides: **households** (Seekers) and **skilled workers** (Professionals).
It follows the launch plan in `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/onboarding.md`:

- **Money is the hook on both sides.** Households see prices before they ask. Workers set their own price and keep all of it.
- **The launch starts in Angono**, with Antipolo and Taytay also open.
- **Workers are Kuya / Ate / Manong / Manang**, never "laborers".
- **Use service words only.** Never "job", "hire", "employ", "employment", "salary" or "minimum wage". Tarbaho is a directory, not an employer.
- **Never vouch for a worker.** Say "what we checked" or "ID checks", never "trusted", "guaranteed" or "safe workers".
- **Short, plain words**, with Taglish next to the English.

Images in this folder (made by `build.js`): `developer-page-header.png` (4096×2304, under 1 MB) and
`developer-page-icon.png` (512×512) for the Play Console **Developer page**.

## Google Play: store listing

**App name (≤ 30):** `Tarbaho: Kuya & Ate nearby`

**Short description (≤ 80):**
> Find a skilled Kuya or Ate nearby, or offer your skills. No commissions.

(Taglish option: `Maghanap ng Kuya o Ate malapit sa'yo, o umextra. Walang komisyon.`)

**Full description:**

> **Tarbaho: skilled Kuya and Ate near you, in Angono, Antipolo and Taytay.**
>
> Is the aircon broken? Need a hand with tiles, plumbing or a roof leak? Need groceries bought or a document picked up? Open Tarbaho and see who is nearby, how much they usually charge, and what other households said about them.
>
> **For households**
> • See the price first. Every Kuya and Ate shows their usual price per service, so you know before you message.
> • Set your budget. Post what you need, add photos, and get offers. Pick the one that fits.
> • Close to home. Results start with your town and barangay.
> • See what we checked. Look for ID, TESDA and past-project badges, real reviews, and photos of their work.
> • Chat in the app. Your number stays private until you pick an offer.
> • Pay after the work is done, at the price you both agreed. No booking fees.
>
> **For skilled workers (Umextra para sa pamilya)**
> • Free to list. We don't take a cut. What the customer pays is yours.
> • Set your own price. You decide your average price per service.
> • Get requests nearby. See what households near you need, and send an offer in one tap.
> • Get seen more. Add work photos and badges (ID, TESDA, past project) to show up higher.
> • No CV and no office visit. Your phone number, a selfie, and you're live.
>
> **Services:** Aircon Technician • Construction (foreman, carpentry, electrical, plumbing, masonry, tiles, welding, roofing & gutter, steelman, helper) • Errands (house cleaning, grocery, bills, PasaBuy, documents, parcels, car cleaning, gardening) • Driver (On-Call) (family driver, out of town, airport transfer, motorcycle rider) • Car Mechanic (Mekaniko) • Pest Control (termites, cockroaches, rats, mosquitoes, bed bugs, ants)
>
> **English or Taglish.** You choose on the first screen.
>
> Tarbaho is a directory: households and workers deal with each other directly. You can report or block anyone, and we answer reports within 5 business days.
