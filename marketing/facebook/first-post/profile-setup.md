## Step-by-step guide (text workers can read)

> Para to pag bukas na ung app. Sa ngayon, fill up mo muna ung early access form: [GOOGLE FORM LINK]

**Step 1. Language tapos sign in**
Buksan mo ung Tarbaho. Pili ka ng **English** o **Taglish**.
Type mo ung **mobile number** mo. May darating na **SMS code**, ilagay mo. (Pwede rin Google sign-in.)

**Step 2. Pili ka ng "Skilled worker"**
Hindi "Household". Ung household, para sa mga nagpapagawa.

**Step 3. Trade mo, tapos services**
Pili ka ng trade mo, hal. **Construction** o **Aircon Technician**.
Tapos tick mo ung services na ginagawa mo, hal. Carpentry, Tiles, Electrical, o Linis ng aircon.
Wala sa list? Pili ka ng **"Others"** tapos type mo ung ginagawa mo.

**Step 4. Presyo mo**
Lagay mo range mo: **from ₱… to ₱…**. O pili ka ng **"depende sa trabaho"**.
Ung household ang magbabayad sayo pagkatapos ng gawa, sa presyong napag-usapan nyo.

**Step 5. San ka pumupunta, ilang taon ka na**
Tick mo lahat ng bayan na kaya mo: **Angono, Taytay, Antipolo**.
Pili ka kung ilang taon ka na sa trabaho.

**Step 6. Selfie at number**
Selfie mo, dapat kita mukha, walang shades. **Kailangan to.**
Confirm mo ung **mobile number** mo sa SMS. **Kailangan to bago ka mag-live.**

**Step 7. Check, promise, Game!**
Check mo ung card mo, yan ung makikita ng mga nagpapagawa.
Tick mo ung promises: tapat sa gawa at presyo, susunod sa Terms at community rules, patas na offer.
Tap mo ung **"Game!"** (sa English: "Ready"). **Live ka na.**

**Dagdag, para mas makita ka (di required)**
- Hanggang **6 na pics** ng mga natapos mong gawa.
- **Badges:** Government ID, TESDA certificate, o past project sa company o developer.
Mas mataas ka sa search pag may pics at badges.

**Pag live ka na**
- Sa **Requests** tab, kita mo ung mga request ng mga nagpapagawa na malapit sayo (may budget, minsan may picture).
- **Offer** ka ng presyo mo, o kunin mo ung budget nila.
- **Chat** muna sa app.
- Ung **number mo**, makikita lang nila **pag pinili ka nila**.

### English gloss of the guide (for the owner)

1. Pick English or Taglish; type your mobile number and enter the SMS code (Google sign-in also works).
2. Pick "Skilled worker", not "Household" (households are the ones who need work done).
3. Pick your trade (e.g. Construction or Aircon Technician), then tick the services you do. Not listed? Pick "Others" and type it.
4. Enter your price range, from ₱… to ₱…, or "depends on the work". The household pays you after the work, at the price you agreed.
5. Tick every town you go to (Angono, Taytay, Antipolo) and pick your years of experience.
6. Take a selfie with your face clearly visible, no shades (required) and confirm your number by SMS (required before going live).
7. Check your card, tick the promises (honest work and prices, Terms and community rules, fair offers), tap "Game!" ("Ready" in English). You're live.
Extras (optional): up to 6 work photos; badges for government ID, TESDA certificate, past project with a company or developer. These rank you higher in search.
Once live: see nearby households' requests in the Requests tab, send an offer, chat in the app; your number is shown only when they pick you.
