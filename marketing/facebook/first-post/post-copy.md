# Tarbaho: first Facebook Page post (workers, early access)

Audience: construction workers (foreman, karpintero, electrical, tubero, mason, tiles, welder, bubong, steelman, helper) and aircon technicians in Angono, Taytay and Antipolo.
Goal: get them to fill the early-access Google Form before the app is out.
Framing: **the app is not on Google Play yet.** It is "malapit na sa Android". Nothing here says "download now".

## Wording check (done)

- No "hiring", "job opening", "employment", "salary", "minimum wage", "download now", no promise of income or guaranteed work.
- "Unang makakatanggap ng requests" is used, never "guaranteed".
- No user counts, no testimonials.
- Workers are Kuya / Ate, never "laborers".
