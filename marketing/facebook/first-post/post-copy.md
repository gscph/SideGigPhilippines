# Tarbaho: first Facebook Page post (workers, early access)

Audience: construction workers (foreman, karpintero, electrical, tubero, mason, tiles, welder, bubong, steelman, helper) and aircon technicians in Angono, Taytay and Antipolo.
Goal: get them to fill the early-access Google Form before the app is out.
Framing: **the app is not on Google Play yet.** Nothing here says "download" or gives a date.

Voice: the way a worker texts a kasama on Facebook: short, literal, casual Taglish ("ung", "pag", "wag", "di pa", "fill up mo lang"). The only humor is the owner's own hook; no jokes, punchlines or comparisons of our own. No ✅ lists, no emoji walls, no ad phrasing. The hooks, the image subtitles and the answer line are the owner's, word for word. No promise about the price model (no "0% commission", "walang komisyon", "sa'yo ang buong bayad", "libre forever").

Images (built by `../build.py` like every other post; the captions of A and B are also in `../POSTS.md`):
